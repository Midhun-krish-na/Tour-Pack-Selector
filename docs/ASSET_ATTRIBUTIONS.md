# Sample Image Sources and Asset Attributions

## 1. Purpose

This document records the source, creator and copyright status of the sample travel photographs referenced by the Travel Package Selector template.

The project follows a conservative asset policy: sample photographs must have an explicitly identified CC0 1.0 or Public Domain status. Images with unclear, non-commercial, attribution-share-alike or otherwise restrictive terms are excluded.

## 4. Usage and Modifications

The image URLs are referenced by the sample package data in `src/data/packages.json`.

The photographs are hosted externally on Wikimedia Commons and are not currently bundled as local image files in the source repository.

The project may crop, resize or otherwise display these photographs through its existing image presentation components. No modifications to the original photographs are currently documented.

Creator and source information is retained here voluntarily for transparency and traceability, even where the applicable licence does not require attribution.

## 5. Important Rights Considerations

CC0 and Public Domain copyright status does not automatically resolve every possible legal issue.

Separate rights may apply to identifiable people, trademarks, logos, private property, cultural property or other protected subject matter depicted in an image.

The image records should be reviewed again if the photographs are downloaded, modified, replaced or redistributed independently.

## 6. Maintenance

Whenever sample images change:

1. Verify the individual image source page.
2. Confirm the exact copyright or licence status.
3. Record the creator and original source where available.
4. Update this inventory.
5. Remove any image whose rights status becomes unclear.

Do not assume that every image hosted on Wikimedia Commons is CC0 or Public Domain. Always check the individual file page.
