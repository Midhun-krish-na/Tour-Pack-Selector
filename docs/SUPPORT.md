# Support Policy

Thank you for purchasing the CRYCIOUS Travel Package Selector.

This document describes the support and update policy included with the product.

## 1. Support Periods

The following support periods begin on the original purchase date:

- **Installation assistance:** 7 calendar days.
- **Bug reporting:** 14 calendar days.

These periods apply to the original product as delivered.

## 2. Included Support

### Installation Assistance — 7 Days

During the first 7 days after purchase, buyers may request assistance with:

- Installing project dependencies.
- Starting the development server.
- Creating a production build.
- Understanding the supplied installation instructions.

### Bug Reporting — 14 Days

During the first 14 days after purchase, buyers may report reproducible defects in the original, unmodified template.

A report should include:

- A description of the problem.
- Steps to reproduce it.
- Relevant error messages.
- Screenshots or other useful evidence.
- Node.js, npm, browser and operating system versions.

Confirmed defects will be reviewed and addressed at the seller's discretion, taking into account their severity and reproducibility.

## 3. Excluded Services

The purchase does not include:

- Custom feature development.
- Buyer-specific design or branding work.
- Customization of modified source code.
- Backend or database development.
- Authentication implementation.
- Payment gateway integration.
- Third-party API integration.
- Hosting or deployment assistance beyond basic installation guidance.
- Ongoing website maintenance.
- One-to-one development or consulting services.

These services are not part of the standard product purchase.

## 4. Updates Policy

The purchase does not include a guaranteed update period or lifetime updates.

Future bug fixes, compatibility improvements and feature updates may be released at the seller's discretion.

Major product editions or separately released products may require a separate purchase.

## 5. Response Expectations

Support requests are handled through the designated email address.

Response times may vary depending on availability and the complexity of the issue. No fixed response-time guarantee is provided.
